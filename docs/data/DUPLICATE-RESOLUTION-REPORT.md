# Duplicate Resolution Report

**Document status:** Government geography, shared-campus cases, and Loveland boundary policy resolved — no recorded duplicate case awaits Product Owner review

## Purpose

This report documents potential duplicate physical destinations, alternate-name questions, relationship conflicts, mailing-address differences, and multi-area coverage cases raised through the Archdiocese, Catholic-web, and government-geography passes. Candidate references are research handles, not canonical church IDs.

## Duplicate-identification rules

- Treat one physical worship destination as one canonical record.
- Review possible duplicate names, addresses, coordinates, organizations, and campus relationships using documented evidence.
- Preserve alternate names and conflicting evidence rather than silently discarding them.
- Do not resolve a possible duplicate by guesswork.

## Parish-versus-church distinctions

A parish organization and a physical church are not automatically the same entity. Resolution decisions must distinguish organizational relationships from physical worship destinations.

## Shared-campus handling

Shared-campus relationships must be documented without automatically combining distinct physical destinations or duplicating one destination. A distinct chapel is separate only when the approved scope and official evidence support it as a distinct physical destination.

## Renamed, merged, closed, and inactive locations

Renamed, merged, closed, and inactive locations may be retained for duplicate resolution or historical clarity, but they must remain clearly distinguishable from active destinations and retain an evidence trail.

## Resolution requirements

Each case must identify both candidate records or evidence representations, the issue type, alternate names or conflicting addresses, their relationship, supporting evidence, the resolution, and its status. Stable identifiers must not be casually reassigned after approval.

## Unresolved-item escalation

Escalate any duplicate, identity, shared-campus, address, renamed, merged, closed, or inactive-location case that authoritative evidence cannot resolve or that requires a Product Owner boundary or modeling decision.
