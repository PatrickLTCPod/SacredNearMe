**Document status:** Archdiocese-only discovery pass completed — candidate sources recorded; no canonical church IDs assigned

## Purpose

This document maps current official Archdiocese evidence to research candidates and will map each accepted canonical church record to its supporting evidence. This pass creates no canonical church record.

## Authority-level guidance

- Apply the approved hierarchy in `DATA_GOVERNANCE.md`.
- Prefer official publishers over secondary directories.
- Treat secondary directories and user reports only as research leads unless governance explicitly permits otherwise.
- Preserve conflicting official information and missing-source conditions.
- Recording a future worship, bulletin, or calendar source does not authorize schedule extraction during Phase 1.

- Parish home page

- Official church or campus page
- Archdiocese directory entry
- Official sacrament or worship page
- Official calendar
- Official bulletin location
- Official contact page
- Official social account only when needed
- Direct-confirmation record

## Conflict and availability notes

- The live Find a Parish page states that the Archdiocese has 205 parishes, while its six linked regional datasets contain 206 feature records in total: Central 18, Northeast 46, Northwest 45, South 47, Southeast 24, and Southwest 26. This pass did not attempt to reconcile the global discrepancy by changing local records.
- The 2026 Parish Vitality report describes Archdiocese-wide canonical unifications and partial unifications. It does not by itself establish any candidate's local parish-versus-physical-destination identity, so no relationship was inferred from the aggregate report.
- Current directory and linked family-page evidence place St. Anthony in South Family 9, while the current 2026 appointment notice lists St. Cecilia and St. Mary but omits St. Anthony. The relationship remains unresolved.
- The current family-name registry has no selected name for Southeast Family 4. St. Columban and St. Margaret of York remain associated only to the family identifier pending later verification.
- Older current-linked family pages were treated as supporting evidence, not automatically as current authority when newer 2026 evidence conflicted.
- Required canonical field semantics remain undefined for `travel_zone` and for the permitted or required values of `location_type`, `active_status`, and `research_status`. No canonical inventory row or `church_id` was created.
